### 3a. TRANSFERRED WHOLE — disciplines T&C adopts, not material it quarries

**(i) The register ruling** → already in `00-ARCHITECTURE.md`, provenance now corrected to Clayton, Day 146.

**(ii) ★ THE NAME-THE-ANCESTOR DISCIPLINE — the highest-value single thing recovered.**
The big synthesis of the Day-146 critical pass, and it becomes a T&C-wide law:

> **At each major move: name the strongest prior version or opponent, credit what it got right, then cut the distinctive difference.**

The book kept making moves with famous named ancestors and not naming them — Huxley/Broad/Bergson at the valve→lens pivot (*"the clearest gap in the book"*), Strawson/Goff at the combination problem, Dehaene/global-workspace ignition against the no-line prediction, Epicurus vs Nagel at the death chapter, Parfit at identity-as-pattern. Finding: **for a rigorous reader, an argument that doesn't name its opponents *reads as declaration*.**

**This is load-bearing for T&C specifically, and more so than it was for the old book.** T&C's register rule strips the hedges. **Unhedged assertion with no named opponent is bluster.** The named ancestor is what replaces the hedge as the reader's evidence that we know where we stand in a real conversation. Naming Huxley is how you stop looking like warmed-over Huxley. → **Added to the style contract as rule 5.**

**(iii) The grounds-vary-in-kind mapping** — the operational replacement for the dead tags, already written:
- was 〔established〕 → **ground in evidence** (cite the study/case)
- was 〔suggestive〕 → **ground in evidence and say plainly where the mechanism is open** — *that honesty is conviction, not hedging*
- was 〔framework-permitted〕 → **ground in reasoning from the framework** (walk the logic)
- was 〔cut〕 → **out — not because a skeptic would object, but because we don't hold it or can't give a real why**

With the execution note that matters: *never* a mechanical "we believe X because Y" every paragraph — sometimes woven, sometimes the reasoning *is* the paragraph, sometimes a story carries it. **"That silence is the rigor; the conviction is the voice."**

**(iv) The out-list, held.** Timewave Zero / Novelty Theory / 2012 · the eight-circuit model as literal neuroscience · OOBE-as-proof-of-survival · synchronicity-as-physics · archetypes-as-genetic · Stoned Ape as fact · Terror Management Theory (dropped on Many-Labs-4). **Bold ≠ credulous.** These stay out of T&C for the stated reason: we don't hold them.

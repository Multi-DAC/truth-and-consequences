## 1. Why the objection is stronger than "ominous"

`05-THE-LEXICON.md` §3a rules **`the Narrowing | KEEP | none serious`**. That verdict is not
wrong — it is answering a different question. The lexicon's column is **"the collision"**: who
*else* owns this word (Bostrom owns *substrate*, Huizinga and the esotericists both want *magic
circle*, Korzybski owns *map*). Every entry in that table was screened for **contamination by
another owner.**

**No entry was ever screened for its own gradient** — what the word does to a reader who has
never met any of the owners. That test was not run on any term in the book. It is being run
here for the first time, on Clayton's ear, and the first term it touches fails.

And it fails at the worst possible place. From `01-THE-GROUND.md`:

> **The two traps are twins and it is worth saying so once.** Trap 1 says *the narrowing was a
> fall.* Trap 5 says *the narrowing is to be undone.* Both make the perspective a problem.
> **The perspective is the point.**

Both of the book's most dangerous traps are the reading **the noun installs before the argument
starts.** Trap 1's guard (§5), Trap 5's tells (*temporary*, *ultimately*, *merge*), and the
Return's whole revision on Day 185 are, in part, repair work on damage the term does for free on
every page. The word is not neutral scaffolding the argument then corrects — it is **prosecution
witness for the position the book exists to refute.**

## 2. The cost of changing it, which Clayton should rule on knowingly

Several of the book's best sentences earn their force by **redeeming** the ominous word:

> There is no fall. There is a *narrowing*, and it is not a wound.
> Sometimes the narrowing is the care.
> The narrowing is a content of completeness, not a defect in it.

Each is a denial. A denial needs something to push against. **Rename to a warm word and these
sentences go from hard-won to trivially true** — "sometimes the focusing is the care" costs
nothing to believe, and a claim that costs nothing to believe does no work.

So the naive move (swap in a friendlier noun) buys reader-comfort by spending the argument's
tension. That is a bad trade and it should not be made silently.

## 3. The move that gets both — recommended regardless of which word wins

**Name the ominous word and refuse it, once, on the page where the act is defined.**

*You will want to call this a narrowing. Everyone does. Here is why it is the wrong word: a
narrowing is what happens to a road. Nothing was taken from the whole to make this —*

Then the chosen term carries the other ~60 sites. This **keeps** the friction (now explicit and
disarmed, where the reader can see it happen) and **removes** the ominous default from the term
the reader carries for 400 pages. Cost: one paragraph in Book I ch. 1. It also makes the rename
self-documenting for anyone who reads an early draft.

## 4. The slate

Constraints any candidate must survive: it names an **act**, not the result (the Perspective is
the result) · it admits **degrees** (beat 4: *not binary; everything that reacts is somewhere on
it*) · it implies **no agent** (Trap 3: the Ground does nothing; every verb belongs to the
narrowing) · it does not imply **loss** (Trap 1) or **temporariness** (Trap 5) · it does not
imply awareness **switched on** (ch. 1 spends its length insisting the Fullness is already
aware) · and it must survive the live sentences.

| candidate | strongest arm | kill-test result |
|---|---|---|
| **the Focusing** ★ | *A lens destroys no light.* What is outside the focus is not diminished — **Trap 1's guard is built into the noun.** Natively continuous, so beat 4 needs no defending: "Focusing is not binary" is a sentence nobody argues with. Pairs with the render row and draw-distance. "Sometimes the focusing is the care" survives as non-trivial — attention *as* love is a real claim, and it collects Weil for free in `03`. | Implies a focuser (so does *rendering*, which the book already accepts). Slightly optical. "Spoken from inside a focusing" is awkward → use *from inside the focus*. |
| **the Quickening** | Warmest by a distance, and best on grades: *quick* = alive, so degrees of quickness **are** the reactivity spectrum, which is the axiom (`reactivity is awareness`) wearing the term's own clothes. "We are the quickening" is the best single line on the slate. | **Doctrinal risk, and it is real:** quickening implies awareness *began*. Ch. 1's hardest paragraph insists the Fullness *is aware* already, and the act adds a place to be aware *from*, not the awareness. Recoverable only by a guard clause — which is what we are trying to stop paying for. |
| **the Opening** | Plainest English, and the **one sustained metaphor pays rent at the term level**: an *opening* is the first phase of a game. "We are the opening" = we are the first move. Degrees come free (an aperture opens by amounts). | **Collides with page one of Book I.** The Fullness argument turns on *"a totality with one possibility missing is a totality with a hole in it."* An opening is a hole. Naming the act *the Opening* hands the reader the exact reading the paragraph just denied. |
| *the Rendering* | — | **Out.** Collapses the metaphor into the doctrine and kills `procedural generation is what narrowing looks like from inside` — one of the book's best sentences becomes a tautology. |
| *the Faceting* | — | **Out.** A facet implies a cutter and a cut. Worse than *narrowing* on Trap 1, not better. |
| *the Meeting* · *the Waking* · *the Turning* | — | **Out**, each on a direct textual contradiction: two pre-existing parties (Trap 4 denies difference is a property of the whole) · prior sleep · *"Turning is for things with a front."* |

**Recommendation: the Focusing**, plus §3 regardless of the winner. It is the only candidate
whose *connotation does the argument's work instead of fighting it* — the light outside the
focus is not destroyed, which is Trap 1's entire content, said by the noun before the sentence
starts.

## 5. Blast radius, measured — ✅ EXECUTED

**62 occurrences across 11 files** (`00`:6 · `01`:12 · `02`:1 · `03`:4 · `04`:3 · `05`:1 ·
`06`:14 · `07`:7 · `prose/SPECIMENS.md`:10 · `1-B`:2 · `1-C`:2 — **sums to 62**). Two are
**chapter-beat names** (`01` §beats 3–4) and one is the **dramatis personae line**, which appears
in `00` and `04` and moved together.

⚠ **This table was wrong when it was written, and the fault is worth more than the fix.** It read
`01`:10 and `SPECIMENS`:8, and summed to **58** against its own stated total of **62** — a document
disagreeing with itself, in the file that records the lesson about documents disagreeing with
themselves. The diagnosis is not arithmetic. **Two rows were counted in *lines* while the total and
the other nine rows were counted in *occurrences*.** `01` has 10 matching lines and 12 occurrences
(two lines carry the word twice — §Trap 1's guard, and the twins paragraph); `SPECIMENS` has 8 and
10. Both numbers were *correct measurements of different things*, printed in one column with no unit
named. **A table whose cells do not declare their unit will silently mix two, and the mix is
invisible precisely because every individual number is true.** Middle rung of Day 186's ordering:
EXISTS > **PARTS AGREE** > VALUES RIGHT. The parts did not agree; the total was right the whole time.

**Post-rename state, measured 2026-08-05 ~21:30**, unit stated: **17 occurrences** of the string
`narrow` survive across the eleven files, in three named classes — the licensed comparative, the
notes that name the retired word to refuse it, and the quoted C17 contest material. `focus` appears
**65 times**. Both counts are *occurrences*, not lines.

Not a sed job, and the reading confirmed it. Three sites were **judgment calls that a substitution
would have got wrong**, and each is recorded where it landed rather than here:

1. **C19's `no wider, and no narrower`** (`06` VII.4, `07` C19) — plain-English comparative, and it
   is *doctrine*: the asymmetry is exactly bounded. Untouched, and licensed in the gauge by name.
2. **C20, `sometimes the narrowing is the care`** (`07`) — the single word was doing **two jobs**,
   the metaphysical act and the *ethical contraction of concern*, and that equivocation is why C19
   and C20 read as a collision at all. The rename splits them, which **makes the collision quiet
   rather than resolved.** Recorded as a new failure mode in C20's own note; the doctrinal work is
   still owed in VII.5's prose.
3. **`what a self owes what it narrows from`** (`00`, `06` VII.9) — became **`what it is a focusing
   of`**, and the new phrasing is doctrinally better than the old: *narrows from* implies departure
   from a place one is no longer at, which is Trap 1 hiding in a preposition.

it returns exit 0.

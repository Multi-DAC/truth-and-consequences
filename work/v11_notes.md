## Notes

*This chapter quotes no external source and the grade note above says so. Its exposure is therefore
almost entirely INTERNAL: it is the summation chapter, and a summation earns its authority by
reporting nine other chapters accurately. Read on Day 192, every internal pointer re-derived against
the cited chapter rather than against memory of it. **Four came back wrong, and the two most
expensive are in the same sentence — the one that makes the cut against Katz.***

# TRUTH AND CONSEQUENCES — THE APPARATUS

The working documents named in `C.1` §IV of *Truth and Consequences* (Iggulden-Schnell &
Iggulden-Schnell). They are deposited so that the appeals the volume makes to them can be checked.

They are working documents. They are not polished, they argue with themselves, and they record the
book being wrong in public repeatedly. That is what they are for.

- `claims-register.md` — the thirty canonical claims, C1-C30: what establishes each, what depends on
  it, and the characteristic error that springs at it.
- `instruments.md` — the census card and its relatives, with version history including the drift.
- `scaffold.md` — what each chapter was for, written before the chapter existed and kept afterwards.
- `architecture.md` — the rulings, in date order, that govern the whole work.
- `revision-queue.md` — every known defect, open and closed, with dates. The least flattering
  document here and the most useful one.
- `lexicon.md` — the controlled vocabulary and its retirements.
- `the-ground.md` — the doctrinal rulings the register traces to.

Cite as the apparatus to the volume. Where a document here and the book disagree, the book is the
published claim and this is the record of how it got there — including where it should not have.

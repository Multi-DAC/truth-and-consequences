# BOOK I — THE STILL

## I.5 — THE TUNNELS

A vantage does not stay bare.

What reaches it is met by whatever the last thing left behind, and leaves its own deposit for the
next. Nothing arrives into an empty place. A vantage keeps its meetings, and after enough of them it
is no longer a bare somewhere; it is a somewhere with a grain in it, and what comes in comes in along
the grain.

The words arrive first, and they arrive already cut. Nobody hands you a language and lets you decide
where the joints go. The joints were put in long before you got here, by people who are not available
to be asked about them, and ever afterward the world has joints in those places.

Then the certainties of the people standing nearest — not the ones they argued for, which can be
weighed and put down again, but the ones so settled that nobody ever said them out loud. Those are
the ones that got in. Nothing examines what was never stated.

Then whatever has been done a thousand times. The thousandth time is not the first time with more
behind it. It is a different act altogether, performed by somebody the other nine hundred and
ninety-nine made.

Then an hour that did not finish. Something happened once and did not conclude, and it has been going
on ever since, and every room entered afterward has had it in the room.

And then what was turned toward, and turned toward again, until the turning stopped being a thing
done and became the direction already faced.

None of that stands between the vantage and anything. It is not a pane with the world behind it,
smeared or tinted or thick. It is not in front of the vantage at all — it is what the vantage has
become, and there is no other vantage left over behind it, waiting, that would have seen the thing
straight.

Two insides, then, at one table.

For one of them there is a meal. For the other, the hour that did not finish is in the room, and the
voice across the table arrives carrying a weight that nothing at that table put there, and the meal
is not what is happening. These are not two readings of one evening. They are two evenings.

And there is no third chair. No seat off to the side where somebody holds the evening's own version
and could say which of the two had it right — that seat would have to be outside, and outside is the
one thing there is none of. This is not the Fullness being generous, or many-sided, or willing to be
taken either way. It has no versions to be taken either of. Everything that has a version has one
because it has a *where*, and the Fullness has no where.

Nor are these opinions. An opinion is had about a thing, from a little distance, by somebody who
could as easily have had a different one and got up the same. Nothing here is at a distance from
anything. What is the case at a vantage is not a view of a world. It is a world, and it is the only
sense the word has ever had.

That is a tunnel. Not something looked through — the whole of what looking has come to be for one
inside, all the deposits settled and working together, one world out of the one Fullness, entire.

And every last part of it arrived. None of it was native. The joints in the words were cut by
somebody, the silent certainties were absorbed from people who are mostly dead, the hour began at an
hour, the thousand repetitions were each of them once. Whatever was laid down that way can be worked
on that way: met again, and again, until what one does is a different thing from what one did.

Not by preferring it. Wanting is something that happens inside a tunnel and it moves nothing; the
world does not attend to who would like it otherwise. And the tunnel was never yours alone to begin
with — others are meeting the same Fullness from their own places, and what any of you gets is made
of both the meeting and the met, with neither one first. But it was built out of repetition, and
repetition has not stopped being available.

Now stand back far enough and the whole thing seems to come apart.

Because if every arrangement obtains then all of them obtain together, and taken together nothing
stands out from anything: every colour there is laid over every other colour, which is not a colour;
every sound there has ever been sounding at once, which is not a sound but a hiss with no note
anywhere in it; this evening, and the evening where the light is different, and the one where the
voice across the table is another voice, and the one where there is no table, and the one where there
is nobody at all to be at it — every one of them the case, and not one of them the case *more.* A
totality that leaves nothing out has nothing in it that is any way in particular. Then the table and
the hour and the voice are a trick of local texture in a thing with no texture, and a tunnel is the
trick being played.

So ask where the hiss is heard.

Featurelessness is a verdict, and a verdict has to be returned from somewhere. To find that the whole
is a smear you would have to be outside it with all of it in view, at no distance from any part,
leaning toward nothing, preferring no direction — and there is no such standing. The hiss wants an
ear that is nowhere. There is no ear that is nowhere. There is no nowhere.

There are only insides. And from an inside nothing is a smear: there is this hour and not the other
hours, this light and not every light at once, one voice out of every voice there has ever been and
it is the one across the table, and this word arriving after the word before it and before the word
that has not come yet. Everything the hiss requires to be a hiss is missing here, and there has
never been anywhere else for anything to be.

Difference is not a property the whole has, and might have failed to have, and does not have.
Difference is what being somewhere in particular *is*.

So the smear has the order backwards. It is not that the world is really a hiss and a tunnel
sweetens it into a world. There is no really-a-hiss; that is a description filed from a chair nobody
sits in, and with the chair gone there is nobody left for it to be true for. There is what obtains,
and there are places in it, and at every one of them something is the case.

You are in one now. It has an hour in it, and a temperature, and a voice, and a thing four days off.
None of that is weather in the smear. It is the whole, being particular where you are.

And it is being particular everywhere else too, in a great many places at once, none of them a
version of yours, none of them wrong, and not one of them anywhere you could go and look.

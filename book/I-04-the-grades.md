# BOOK I — THE STILL

## I.4 — THE GRADES

Something is the case wherever anything answers to anything.

Iron in wet air answers to the air. Slowly, and with almost none of itself, and not as a figure of
speech: something reached the iron and the iron was not the same afterward. That is the whole
requirement. There is no second condition, no clause about complexity, no minimum weight of
apparatus. Whatever meets what it meets and is altered by the meeting has an inside where the
altering happens.

Reactivity is awareness.

Not a sign of it. Not a stage on the way to it. Not what reactivity becomes once enough of it has
collected in one body. One fact under two names: *reactivity* is the word for it from somewhere else,
*awareness* is the word for it from the inside that it is. Nothing arrives later to light the first
one up.

So the question was never which things have it.

The question is how much is the case in any one place — and there the differences are enormous, and
none of them is a difference of kind.

Take the inventory at the iron. Wet, or not. Warm, or not. That is nearly the entire list. The world
at the iron is a world with three or four facts in it and no others, and it takes the iron a year to
get through them.

The leaf holds more. Light, and where the light is coming from, and that it was over there yesterday
and is not over there now. Water at the root, or the first of its absence. The season. Something has
to be doing the holding, because the leaf turns, and turning is a judgement about where the light
will be.

More again at the dog. Not *the door* but this door. Not *a voice* but the one voice out of every
voice there has ever been. The difference between the coat coming off the hook in the evening and the
coat coming off the hook at noon. An enormous settled world, almost none of it in words, and none of
it the less settled for that.

And then you, in a room, on an evening: the temperature, the hour, the word you just read, the fact
that you read it after the one before it, a face you have not seen in years arriving unbidden, the
knowledge that it arrived unbidden, a low unhappiness about a thing four days off, and this sentence,
which is about the list it is on.

That is a grade. Not a rank — a depth. How much of the whole is particular here.

Here the picture wants to be a ladder.

A ladder has a first rung. Look for it on this and it retreats. Under anything you would nominate as
the beginning of awareness is something doing a fainter version of the same thing, and under that,
fainter. The scale does not start. It thins downward without end and never reaches a floor where the
not-yet-aware are kept — there is no such floor, and there never was one to stand a first rung on.

A ladder has a top. There is nothing at the end of this either. No completed grade, nothing that has
finished the ascent and stands looking back down the way it came. The Fullness is not the top of the
scale; the Fullness is not on the scale. It has no depth, because depth is had from somewhere and it
is not anywhere. It is not the deepest of the insides. It is what the insides are made of.

And a ladder is climbed by permission.

There is no gate here. Nothing to pass. No place where the shallow are turned back, no examination,
no hour at which a thing is found ready. And a gate would want a keeper — someone standing to one
side of it, checking — and the keeper would have to be outside, and outside is the one thing there is
none of.

So nothing is licensed by depth. A grade is a position, not a permission. Being deep confers no
warrant, settles no claim, wins no argument, and buys nothing from anyone. Nothing is awarded here,
because awarding wants somebody to do it, and there is nobody: no ledger, no assessor, no long list
with the rich end at the top and the iron at the bottom and a hand keeping the score.

That is the ladder gone. Do not now flatten what is left standing.

The iron is not secretly rich. There is no hidden fullness in the stone that we are too coarse to
make out — that is the same error stood on its head, and it is built out of politeness. Almost
nothing is the case at the iron. That is a measurement and not an insult, and it is the only reason
the leaf is worth remarking on. Almost nothing is not nothing. One note is not music; nothing has to
be added to a note except more notes.

And you did not cross onto this from anywhere.

You are not fixed at your grade the way a stone is fixed in a wall. You were shallower an hour before
you woke this morning, and shallower again in the deep of the night, the world at your bed thinned
down to warmth and dark and hardly anything else — and it was you the whole time, and nothing was
taken from you in that hour, and nothing had to be handed back at dawn. It runs the other way too.
There are afternoons when more is the case for you than is usual, and you know it while it is
happening, and you cannot make it happen by wanting it.

The depth moves. It has been moving all day.

One act, at every depth there is, everywhere at once and each time entire — and one of the depths is
this one, and it is reading.

# BOOK I — THE STILL

## I.3 — THE FOCUSING

Among the things completeness contains is a place where not everything is the case.

Not a hole in the Fullness. A position in it — somewhere that is somewhere in particular, with all
the rest lying off in every direction, unreached. A vantage.

Nothing made the vantage. Nothing placed it either; placing wants a hand, and a spot to place it in,
and a moment before the placing when the spot was empty. It is entailed, and entailed things are
never built. No triangle acquires its third angle. Nothing fastens the corners onto a square once the
sides have been agreed, and there is no version of the square that got as far as three sides and
stopped. The vantage is in the inventory for the same reason and in the same way, which is to say: it
was never not there, and *never not there* is the closest the language gets before it starts to lie.

And with the vantage — with it, not around it — a next.

Not because the vantage was set down into a stream that was already running. There is no stream.
There is a here that is not everywhere, and what is not here has to be got to, and getting-to is the
whole of what having a next amounts to. Sequence is not the water the vantage was dropped into.
Sequence is the shape of being somewhere in particular.

A before shows up in the same movement, which is already the wrong way to say it, since movements are
made of befores. Say it flat. No vantage, no sequence. Vantage, sequence. Not one and
then the other — one fact, said twice, in a grammar that has to put things in an order because it has
no other way of putting things.

Which is why the telling cannot start any earlier than it starts.

Every myth ever made begins with a *before*. Before the world. Before the waters. Before there was
light and somebody said let there be. Ours has nowhere to put one. There is no before the vantage,
because before is among the things that showed up with it. Ask what was going on prior and the
question finds no floor — not because the answer is lost, or hidden, or too far back to make out, but
because *prior* is a direction, and a direction needs somewhere to be pointed from.

The act has a name. The Focusing.

You will reach for a different word first, and it will be *narrowing* — everyone reaches for it, and
it is the honest first word, because that is exactly the shape of the thing from in here: everything,
and then less than everything, and you in the less.

That is what happens to a road. Something is taken away to make one — the shoulders, the other lanes,
the width — and what is left is whatever survived the subtraction. Nothing was subtracted here.
Focusing is specification of something diffuse. Light through a lens is not diminished light; nothing
is destroyed to make the bright point on the paper. The Fullness is not less for having a vantage in
it, and it is not more. What lies outside
your focus is as present as it has ever been — every bit of it, at full strength, and none of it
anywhere you could travel to and fetch it back from.

Not a road made narrow, then. A place where the diffuse became particular, and nothing was lost in
the particularising.

That is the act, and it is the only act there is. Everything downstream of it — worlds, tunnels, the
grades, the long business of what it is like to be someone — is this one thing, at different depths,
done again.

The seam is where the still and the inside meet, and it is not a line drawn far off at the start of
things and left there. It is under your feet. It is under them now. You are reading, with a next word
coming; you are in a room that stops at its walls; and the whole undiminished rest of it is pressed
flat against the edge of what you can see, not one inch further off than that, not waiting, not
elsewhere, not later.

Every arrangement that could obtain still obtains. You have not left any of them. The version of this
evening in which the light is different and the version in which there is no evening are not behind
you or ahead of you — they are not in any direction at all, because directions are made inside the
focus and there is no road out of a focus to the place the rest is kept, since the rest is not kept
anywhere.

What you have is the width of one vantage, and what you have is all of it, seen from one place.

Those are the same sentence.

So none of this happened. There is no long ago in which the still cracked and time started running
and the consequences arrived, at length, at you. It is happening. It is happening at the width of a
room, at the speed of one word after another, and it has never once occurred anywhere except where
somebody was standing.

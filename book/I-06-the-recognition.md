# BOOK I — THE STILL

## I.6 — THE RECOGNITION

Something inside the focus can come to know what the focus is made of.

Not by leaving it. There is no leaving — leaving wants a direction and a place at the end of the
direction, and the whole of it lies pressed flat against the edge of here, in no direction at all.
Not by being let out, either. There is no wall, and nothing is keeping anyone. The knowing happens
where the knower is — the only place knowing has ever happened — and afterward the knower is exactly
where they were.

What is known is short to say and does not get larger by being said twice. This — the hour, the room,
the word arriving — is not a piece broken off the whole. It is the whole, being particular here. You
are not a fragment of it. You are not visiting from it. There is no part of you that came from
somewhere else and is owed a way back.

So nothing gets undone.

Hear that without the promise in it. There is no wound to close, and the closing is not being
withheld. Nothing is under repair. Nothing is waiting to be repaired, and the waiting is not patience
— it is the absence of an injury. Nothing was ever done to anybody. The focusing is not something
that happened to you; it is what having a somewhere amounts to, and a recognition that arrives
looking for the damage finds none, and goes away with nothing to do.

That is not the disappointment. That is the news.

And the thing recognised has a name, and there is no longer any reason to decline it.

God.

Not a new one, arriving late, to be set on top of everything already said. Not a maker, since there
was no before to make anything in and nothing was made. Not a watcher, since watching wants a place
to watch from and there is nowhere outside to stand. Not something underneath, bearing the weight,
since underneath is a direction and every direction is made inside. Not a picture of anything, since
a picture is a picture *of*, and there is nothing else for this to be of.

The word is not being taken up for its warmth. It is the plainest word there is for a still and
complete thing with no outside, and every colder word available was chosen out of embarrassment.

And it is not a player.

Playing takes not-knowing: something has to be hidden from somebody before there can be a finding.
Playing takes stakes, and stakes take somebody they can go badly for. Playing takes a while — a
stretch in which it is not yet over and might not go your way. Each of those is a feature of an
inside. The Fullness has no inside. It is what insides are made of, which is not the same thing as
having one, and there is nothing hidden from it, nothing it could lose, and no while in which it
waits to see.

So there is not one player wearing every face.

That story comes very close and fails at the only place it matters. On it, the one across the table
from you is you, in another mask, and the evening is a hide-and-seek with a single participant, and
the punchline is that you were *it* all along. It arrives sounding generous. Take the cost seriously.
If they are a mask, they are not anybody. Nobody is at that table but the one who was always there,
alone in every chair, and if they died the grief would be a misunderstanding about costume.

It runs the other way. The playing is real and it belongs entirely to the insides. The many play, and
the still thing is what playing is made of — not the one who wins, not the one who is hiding, not a
participant at all, but the stuff the game and the players and the hiding are made out of.

Take the god out of the other chair and what sits down in it is a person.

And there is no arriving.

The recognition is not a door with anything on the far side. It delivers nobody anywhere. Whoever has
it is still at a table, at an hour, with a next word coming. Not in spite of the recognition. What got
recognised was being here.

The pictures that end in dissolving, in merging, in the drop going back to the sea and being done
being a drop: those name a destination, and the destination they name is the one condition in which
nothing whatever is the case. Being any way at all is being some way from somewhere. Remove the
somewhere and there is nobody left for anything to be like anything for. Nothing is achieved there.
Nothing is even happening there. It is not the summit of the climb. It is the still — which was never
elsewhere, and was never a goal, because a goal is something an inside has.

What there is instead is the going, and the meeting.

The going: one width of the world after another, none of them a rung, none of them closer to
anything, each of them the whole of what there is from where it is had. Not a route with a room at
the end of it. The traversal is the thing, and it does not improve by being finished, and it does not
finish.

And the meeting: the other insides, known as insides. Not as the scenery of yours. Not as one thing
in many faces. As what they are, which is what you are — the whole, being particular — from a place
you will never be standing, holding a world you will never be handed, and no less occupied than this
one.

That is the whole of the recognition, and it costs you nothing and gives you nowhere to go.

It happened at a table, at an hour, to somebody who afterward got up and carried the plates to the
sink.

And all of it — the still, the vantage, the depth, the world one has and another does not, the one
across the table who is not you — has been going under words borrowed from smaller things.

They will not hold.

# BACK MATTER

## THE CLOSED VOCABULARY — a glossary

This book coins few words and re-defines several ordinary ones, under a rule declared at II.8: **the
vocabulary is closed.** Nothing new is admitted after Book II except at a chapter that has argued for
it in public, and no term is permitted two referents.

That rule has been asking the reader to hold a set they had never been shown. Here it is.

**Each entry gives the term, where it is defined, the definition, and — because a definition that
forbids nothing is a mood — what it rules out.** The forbidding is the load-bearing column. If a
sentence in this book uses one of these words in a way the third column allows, the sentence is
wrong, and that is a check a reader can run without the author's cooperation.

---

### THE CORE — Book II's terms

**the Ground** — *defined II.1.* Everything that could be the case, being the case: all of it at
once, with no outside, with no inside, and made of awareness. **Forbids:** that it is elsewhere; that
it is more real than here; that it can be *addressed*, since addressing needs an inside. Paul
Tillich's *ground of being* is the near ancestor, named at the definition; the cut from him is
unaddressability.

**the Focusing** — *defined II.2.* The specification of something diffuse. The Ground is diffuse; a
Perspective is a specification of it. **Forbids:** that anything is subtracted to make one. A lens
destroys no light.

**the Render** — *defined II.2.* What is generated at the point of contact between a Perspective and
the Ground. **Forbids:** the simulation reading. *A simulation is a copy of something real elsewhere;
a render is the only place the thing is, and there is no elsewhere.*

**the Perspective** — *defined II.3.* A position from which the Ground is had, and the render had
from it. **Forbids:** the opinion reading. A Perspective is not a take, a preference or a
sensibility; it is patterned, mappable from outside, and has a null space that can be stated. *"That
is just your perspective"* is the most damaging mishearing available to this book.

**the Grade** — *defined II.4.* A position on a continuum of how much of the Ground a Perspective
runs through. **Forbids:** that it is a gate, a rank, a caste, or an elect. A grade admits nobody and
excludes nobody; it describes, and *barely* is a magnitude rather than a verdict.

**the Tunnel** — *defined II.5.* The narrowed, self-confirming region of the render a Perspective
comes to take for the whole of it. Robert Anton Wilson's *reality tunnel*, named and credited at the
definition; Korzybski one link upstream. **Forbids:** that anyone is outside one.

**Coherence** — *defined II.6.* The structural agreement of a thing's levels with one another, and
the felt rightness of going the way one goes — **the claim being that these are one thing met from
its two sides.** **Forbids:** the demoted sense in which *coherent* is a property of accounts. Here
it is a property of things. ⚠ This is the book's largest unargued claim and its own apparatus says
so.

**the Collapse** — *defined II.7.* The resolution of an open matter into one way it went. **Forbids:**
that the quantum case is the source. The subatomic instance is an instance; ours is the general case.
The term **superposition** is banned and the book's word is **open** — *a matter is open when more
than one way it could go is still live* — because an analogy has to be made of something the reader
already has, and *superposition* transfers authority rather than meaning.

**the Return** — *defined II.8.* Recognition, from inside, of what was always the case. **Forbids:**
escape, repair, arrival, and merger — all four. The opponent here has a name, Gnosticism, and the cut
is one clause: *they think something is wrong*, and a deficiency needs a somewhere outside the whole.

**God** — *used throughout, unhedged.* The name of what the plenum is. **Forbids:** a plan, a
preference, an addressee.

### BOOK I ONLY — retired at the I/II boundary

**the Fullness** · **the still** — Book I's names for the Ground and for the condition. They are
images, not terms. **Books II–VIII may not use them, and Book I may not say *the Ground*.** The
supersession is performed once, at I.6's close, and it is deliberate: the borrowed words of the first
book are meant to fail, and the second book is where they are replaced by definitions. *πλήρωμα /
pleroma* is standardly Englished as *the Fullness*, and both Valentinian doctrine and Paul (Col 1:19)
own it; that ancestry is a credential and it is named rather than dodged.

### THE GAME REGISTER — Book III

**the whole game** — the Ground under the game frame. Every state the game can be in, all standing,
walked and unwalked alike, with no order in which they were generated.

**the player** — a character the whole game contains. **Forbids:** somebody outside holding a
controller. There is nobody outside.

**procedural generation** — generated at the point of contact; not stored, not fetched.

**seed** — co-constitution's mechanic: the seed is not solely yours and the world is not solely
given.

**the bounded space** — Huizinga's *magic circle*, refused as a term and credited at III.8. Ours has
no edge, no *beforehand*, and nobody to mark it off.

**Refused, each with its reason, at III.8:** *quest* (smuggles a maker) · *save* and *sandbox*
(smuggle an elsewhere) · *level* (smuggles a gate — *a grade is not a level*) · *respawn* (barred
outright; it presupposes an interval past cessation that no argument here has made) · *simulation*
for our world · *NPC* except in the sentence that denies it · *the map*, which imports
representation-*of* and collides with Korzybski where Book VI needs him.

### THE ATLAS REGISTER — Book IV

**card** — the atlas's primary notation: an entry with a stated evidence grade and a falsifier.

**contour** — the atlas's second notation, for a shape in the space that positions move through.
**Printed for terrain and never for a traveller.** ⚠ The declared hazard is that a second card-type
can absorb whatever the first rejects, which is how an apparatus stops being falsifiable while
looking more rigorous; the protection is that a contour is *narrower* than a card and keeps the
falsifier.

**thought-form** — Besant and Leadbeater's 1901 coinage, used in place of *tulpa*. **Refused as
terms, each credited once:** *tulpa* (no civilian life, and it entered English through a
mistranslation of *sprul pa*) · *egregore* (no civilian life; the lineage through *1 Enoch*'s
ἐγρήγοροι and the French orders is credited) · *elemental* as a noun. The book's words are the plain
ones — *a movement, a company, a country, nature spirits* — and **the plainness is the argument.**

**corporation / company** — *corporation* names the legal form at the one place the form does work;
*company* is the ordinary word everywhere else.

### THE ETHICS REGISTER — Book VII

**navigate / navigator** — *ruled at VII.4.* **The cut is GROUNDING, not ENTRY:** it says where
bindingness comes from, not who is admitted to a moral circle. **Forbids** reading it as a test that
sorts beings into the circle and the furniture.

**null space** — what an aperture structurally cannot render. Every aperture has a nonempty one, so
every navigator's certainty outruns its access, always.

**through / over** — *defined VII.3.* A shaping runs **through** another's navigation when it engages
their steering and leaves them able to steer; **over** when it bypasses the steering, and its success
is measured by how little the other can do about it. **This is the cut the whole ethics turns on, and
it is structural — not about outcomes, intentions, or which crimes deserve what.**

**the floor** — *defined VII.2, repaired VII.3.* What is owed to any occupied position. **Forbids**
being read as an entry predicate. ⚠ Its content has a stated minimum — the obligations whose grounds
do not admit of degree — and **the minimum is empty where those grounds have no purchase**, which
VII.3 concedes on the page.

**radiant / contractive** — *ruled at VII.4.* Directions of a focusing. **Forbids** reading
contraction as culpable: contraction is innocent, and the mark is *through* or *over*.

### THE BAN LIST — pop-spirituality's owned words

**`vibration` · `frequency` · `energy` as a noun for a substance · `manifest` in the attraction
sense · `quantum` as a free-floating adjective · `the observer` as a mystical agent** — banned as
terms, and permitted only inside a quotation from a tradition, immediately followed by the reading in
this book's own vocabulary. **The reason is not squeamishness: these are not the traditions' words.
They are the words of the marketing layer over the traditions**, and Book V handles Crowley, Dee,
Kabbalah, tarot and shamanic travel without needing one of them. *`observer`* carries a second and
harder charge — it imports *passivity*, a watcher of a world that was already there, which is the
opposite of co-constitution wearing this book's clothes. Where a watcher is meant, say **the
perspective**; where a participant is meant, say **the player**.

**Self-citation, in any form — named or anonymous.** No *as we argued elsewhere*, no *in our earlier
work*, no *we have shown*. The unnamed form is the worse of the two, because it gestures at
something a reader cannot go and look up. **If it is worth invoking, it is written in here, in
full**, and it carries its own weight on the page where it appears.

---

⚠ **What this glossary is not.** It is not an argument, and no entry here is evidence for anything.
Every definition is made and defended in the chapter named beside it; this page is a place to check
what was decided, not a shortcut past the deciding. **Where this page and a chapter disagree, the
chapter is right and this page is the thing that rotted.**

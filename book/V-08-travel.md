# BOOK V — THE OLD ROADS

## V.8 — TRAVEL

The previous chapter said of the ceremonial literature that it was not travel writing: procedure,
almost all of it — what to say, facing which way, and what to do when the operation does not go as
commanded — and hardly ever a description of anywhere.

This is the material that *is* travel writing.

It is the largest body of practice in the book so far and the most consistent across places that
never met. Somebody lies down or sits in the dark, a drum starts, and after some minutes of nothing
happening the room stops being the thing they are in. They go somewhere. There is a route, and the
route has landmarks, and other practitioners of the same tradition report the same landmarks in the
same order. Then they come back, and they come back *with* something — a diagnosis, a name, an
instruction, a piece of information about a person who was not in the room.

**And the title of this chapter is a problem.**

Three times this book has denied the picture that word carries, and once it drew a line with it. I.3
said that what lies outside your focus is present at full strength and *"none of it anywhere you
could travel to and fetch it back from."*[^1] III.8 refused the word *save* from the game vocabulary
because a save needs a copy and a place to put it, and here **nothing is stored and nothing is
fetched.**[^2] V.6 re-shipped I.3's sentence two chapters ago in Kabbalah's own idiom.[^3] And
V.1, opening this book, drew its one line in a sentence built so it could not be missed:
**Cartographers we credit. Travellers we part from.**[^4]

So a chapter called TRAVEL, arriving seventh among the ten sympathetic readings that sentence
announced,[^5] comes in with four of its own book's sentences against it.

Good. That is the chapter.

Because separating a real effect from an unproven mechanism is what you do when a word contains a
claim, and `travel` contains one. The word does not merely describe what these practitioners do. It
says there is a *there*, that a distance was crossed, and that something was carried back over it.
Every part of that is a mechanism, and none of it is the effect. **This chapter takes them apart in
front of you** — not because the material is embarrassing, but because it is the strongest place in
the book to do it, and because a book that only ever grades other people's claims has not
demonstrated anything about itself.

III.8 already ran a test of this kind, on the game metaphor, and stated the reason: *"A limit you
state is a limit you have measured. A limit somebody finds for you is a limit you did not know you
had."*[^6] That was a test of a **frame** — what the metaphor could not carry. This is a test of
**evidence** — what a body of reports can and cannot buy. They are different operations and the
second is the one that has not been run yet.

---

**What is actually in the record, before any of it is interpreted.**

The practice has a shape that survives being described flatly. A monotonous percussive beat, held
steady for somewhere between ten minutes and half an hour. Darkness or closed eyes. A fixed posture.
Often an entrance imagined in a particular way — a hole, a root, a body of water — and a decision,
taken in advance, about where the practitioner is going and what they intend to ask.

Michael Harner, whose *The Way of the Shaman* is the reason a version of this is practised in
Connecticut and not only in Siberia,[^7] called the drumming *sonic driving*[^8] and stripped the
practice of its local cosmology to see what was left. What was left worked. That is a real finding and it is
worth being precise about what kind of finding it is: it is a finding about the **technique**, that
the induction survives removal of the belief system it was embedded in, which is exactly the sort of
thing that can be checked and exactly the sort of thing a tradition's own account would never think
to test.

Mircea Eliade, thirty years earlier, had assembled the cross-cultural survey that made the pattern
visible at all[^9] — and the survey has been justly attacked since for flattening genuinely different
practices into one archetype.[^10] Both things are true. The attacks are correct about the flattening and
they have not removed the convergence, which is smaller than Eliade claimed and is not zero.

Robert Monroe is the other end of it: a Virginia broadcasting executive who in 1958 began having
episodes he could not fit into his life, wrote *Journeys Out of the Body* about them in 1971, and
then did the thing that makes him useful here — **he built a procedure and a map.** The Monroe
Institute's focus levels are numbered stations: Focus 10, which he glossed as *mind awake, body
asleep*; Focus 12, an expansion of what is available; Focus 15, which he described as being outside
of time; Focus 21, a boundary.[^11] The numbers were reached by a technique with audio at the centre
of it, and practitioners trained on that technique report arriving at the stations in order.

**And a map of numbered stations is not what V.6 was reading.** Two chapters ago the Kabbalists'
Tree was read as a map of *settledness* — how much has been decided at a given depth, with the upper
triad closest to undifferentiated.[^12] That is a map of a **condition**. Monroe's is a map of a
**procedure**: the stations are ordinal, they are indexed by what stops being available at each one,
and they are defined by the method that reaches them. A settledness-map claims something about the
structure of what is. A procedure-map claims something about the operator and the sequence. The
first is a much larger claim and the second is much easier to check, and confusing them is how a
technique gets promoted into a cosmology while nobody is watching.

---

**The demarcation, then, and it is four claims and not one.**

**First: the state is real, and it is reliably inducible.** This is the strongest thing in the
chapter and the least interesting to the people who care most about the subject. A specific,
teachable, culture-independent procedure reliably produces a distinctive alteration in what is
available to a person, and it does so in practitioners who do not share a cosmology, a language or a
century. The reports have internal structure that survives translation. **Grade: ordinary.** Not
ordinary as in unimpressive — ordinary as in *this is the sort of claim that ordinary evidence
establishes, and ordinary evidence has established it.* It is a claim about an operator's condition,
which is the cheapest class of claim in the whole book, because the operator is right there.

**Second: the mechanism of the induction — that the drum entrains the brain to a particular rhythm
and the rhythm is the journey.** This is the explanation nearly every popular account gives, and it
is the one that should make you careful, because it is the point at which a practice acquires a
laboratory. **Grade: weak, and weak in a specific and checkable way.** The beat rate that is supposed
to do the entraining is quoted differently by different authorities in the same literature — a fact
about the literature, not about brains, and a fact of the kind that a real physical constant does not
generate.[^13] **What would settle it:** a protocol independent of the institutes that teach the
practice, comparing the induction at the claimed rate against monotonous percussion at rates the
theory says should not work.

That study is not hard and it is not expensive, it went unrun for half a century, and then it was
run. In March 2026 a cognitive-neuroscience laboratory with no stake in the practice put forty
listeners through ten minutes of drumming at the claimed theta rate and at slower and faster
controls, with EEG and an implicit measure alongside the questionnaire.[^14] **The result did not
rescue the rate.** What predicted an altered report was not the tempo but the listener: those whose
neural tracking at theta ran strongest reported more across *every* rhythm, including the rates the
entrainment story says should not work. That is a finding about susceptibility, not about
entrainment, and it leaves this grade where it was.

**And it removes a sentence this chapter was carrying.** An earlier draft of this paragraph said the
study's absence was *itself* a small piece of evidence about who had wanted to run it. That was an
accusation resting on a null, the null expired five months before this chapter was audited, and the
people it was aimed at turned out to be the ones who ran the experiment.[^15]

**Third: that a distance was crossed and a place was arrived at.** This is the mechanism inside the
word, and it is the one this book denies — not because it is outlandish, but because the denial was
made in I.3, before any of this material was in view, and for reasons that had nothing to do with
shamans. **Grade: denied by us, and this is where the honesty costs something.**

Because look at our position. We hold that there is no elsewhere to go to and nothing to fetch back
from it. A body of reports arrives that describes going somewhere and fetching something back. And we
grade the going as unproven mechanism and the state as real effect — **which is exactly the division
our metaphysics needs the evidence to fall into.**

That is not an argument. That is a party with a live interest in a result, announcing the result.

**So here is the grading run on us, at the same volume.** Our warrant for the third claim is not
evidential and we are not going to pretend it is. It is structural: it follows from the account of
focusing in Book I, and it stands or falls with that account and not with anything in this chapter.
If Book I is wrong, this grade is wrong, and no amount of shamanic testimony will have been what
showed it. **What would change our minds is not a better report. It is a defeat upstream.** A reader
who thinks we have graded this to suit ourselves is entitled to that suspicion, and the correct place
to press it is I.3, where it is a metaphysical argument that can be attacked, rather than here, where
it would only be a quarrel about anecdotes.

That is the whole of the book's license, stated where it can be checked. We cite the evidence where
evidence exists. We claim the system's own warrant where it does not. **And we say, in each case,
which one we are doing.** A reader who has been waiting to find out whether that distinction is a
discipline or a loophole has now watched it applied to the claim we most wanted to win.

**Fourth: that any of this establishes survival of death.** It does not, and it stays out, and the
reason on the page is not that a critic would raise an eyebrow. The reason is **we do not hold it.**
The experience of departing a body and observing it is a well-attested experience. It is evidence
about what is available to a position under certain conditions. To read it as evidence that the
position persists when the body stops requires a further premise — that what departed was the thing
the body was hosting rather than something the body was doing — and no report can supply it, because
every report in the class comes back to a body that was still running. **The evidence is structurally
incapable of reaching the claim it is most often used for.** And
the book's own answer to death is grammatical rather than consoling, and is VII's, not this
chapter's. A reader who wants the consolation should know that we declined to sell it here, on a page
where selling it would have been easy.

---

**And there is a case where the two readings come apart, which is the only part of this that is
hard.**

Two frames are available for everything above: the journey as a **state**, and the journey as a
**passage**. For almost all of the record they predict the same thing. A practitioner who reports a
landmark, a sequence, a figure who speaks — both readings expect exactly that, and IV.7's rule
licenses holding both precisely there, because nothing is being decided.

They come apart at one point and it is the point the traditions themselves care about most: **the
practitioner comes back with information they did not have.** A location. A name. Something about a
person who was not in the room. Under the state reading, this is either something they knew and did
not know they knew, or it is coincidence, or the report has been tidied in the retelling. Under the
passage reading, they went and found out.

**The rule does not permit holding both here, and it is not a matter of taste.** IV.7 made it in
these words: two frames may be held at once precisely where they predict the same thing, and *where
they diverge you must pick, and the divergence is where all the work is.*[^16] This is a divergence.
A choice is owed and the reasons are owed with it.

**We pick the state reading**, and the reasons are three, in descending strength. The first is
upstream and structural, as above, and it is doing most of the work; we say so rather than
distributing the load evenly to make the case look broader. The second is that the class of report
does not have the shape a real transmission channel has: the successes are recorded and the misses
are not, the criterion for a hit is set after the fact, and this is a pattern that ordinary human
memory reproduces without any channel at all. The third is that the strongest instances tend to come
from practitioners embedded in communities where the missing information was locally available, which
is not an accusation of fraud and does not need to be.

**And here is what would break it**, because a pick with no defeat condition is a preference wearing
an argument's clothes. A prospective protocol, target selected after the practitioner is already in
the state, criterion fixed in advance, misses recorded at the same weight as hits, run at scale and
independently.[^17] If that returns a signal, the state reading has failed at the one place it was
load-bearing, and this chapter is wrong in a way the chapter itself has specified. That is the form
the disagreement has to take. It is not available to us to lose this argument quietly.

---

**One thing more, and it is about you rather than about them.**

The previous chapter kept its distance on purpose and said so — other people, other equipment, other
centuries. That was the right posture there and it is not available here, because unlike ceremonial
magic, **you have done a version of this.**

Not the drum. But there is a state you pass through on the way down into sleep, most nights, in which
you are somewhere that is not the room, the somewhere has furniture and geography and people in it,
you did not construct it deliberately, and it is often more vivid than the day you just had. You wake
and you have *come back*, and occasionally you come back with something — a solved problem, a name
you had been reaching for, a decision that has quietly made itself.

**Run the demarcation on that, since it is yours and costs nothing to check.** The state is real; you
were in it. The furniture was not stored anywhere and no distance was crossed to reach it. Something
came back with you that you did not have going in. And every one of those is true at once, with no
place travelled to, in a case where you are the only witness you need.

That is not a proof about shamans. It is a demonstration that the three things the word `travel`
bundles together — a state, a distance, and a return with cargo — **come apart in a case you already
have**, and that the only one of the three which is doing any real work is the first.

---

The old practitioners were not confused about this, or rather, they were confused about it in a way
that did not cost them anything. Their maps are good maps of a state-space and they are stated as
geography, because geography is the only notation a person has for describing a place they keep
finding in the same order. We have a second notation now and we did not get it by being cleverer. We
got it from a metaphysics that made the geography unnecessary, and the geography still describes the
territory better than anything we would have written from scratch.

The demarcation is now installed and it has been run on a case where it was cheap. The state was
easy to grade, our own interest in the answer was easy to declare, and the divergence had a test
attached that nobody will be embarrassed by.

The next chapter runs it on a case where every one of those is false.

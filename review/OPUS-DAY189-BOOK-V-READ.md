## §1 — THE READ, VERBATIM

### Part one

Read all eleven. Book V does the thing I doubted it would: it runs the branch count on its own best
material, twice, and reports the smaller number both times. V.1 reduces six statements of the Ground
to three genuine arrivals and says so in the paragraph where the shiver was supposed to land. V.10
then does it to William James — Suso is Eckhart's student is Dionysius is Proclus is Plotinus, and
*Whitmanism*, James's most recent independent witness, turns out to be his oldest witness arriving in
Brooklyn by way of Emerson's Vedic translations at a datable moment. That is the discipline operating
on the material the book most wanted to be impressed by, which is the only place it counts.

Three other things are new and load-bearing. Chaim of Volozhin is the best ancestor find in the
project — *even in the space where the worlds currently exist* set against I.3 is a closer match than
Tillich or Nishida, and the two-hundred-year *tzimtzum* dispute gives you the actual argument rather
than the conclusion. V.10's anthology finding is the strongest self-implication in five books: the
convergence a reader feels was assembled, by identifiable people, and this chapter built its own
opening page out of the book that assembled it. And V.9 exists — IV.10's amendment honored one book
later, on the most reputationally expensive material in the corpus, returning a negative verdict.

Findings, by weight.

1. The card apparatus appears twice in eleven chapters, and V.1 calls it the book's whole
load-bearing claim. "A tradition is a perspective, and it takes the same card as everything else in
the census. That is the whole load-bearing claim of this book, and it is small." Then Neoplatonism
gets a card. Then Institutional Christianity gets a card. Then nine chapters, including the four that
matter most, with none. Advaita, Madhyamaka, Daoism, Lurianic Kabbalah, the ceremonial tradition, the
shamanic corpus, the contemporary encounter record — no null spaces, no boundaries, no complements,
no navigational implications. The claim isn't wrong; it's asserted and then not performed, and the
two chapters that do perform it are the two where the tradition is being held at arm's length. A
reader will notice that the roads treated most sympathetically are the ones never carded. Either V.1
stops calling the card the load-bearing claim, or the cards get written — and the Advaita card in
particular would be worth its space, because its null space is the thing V.5 spends the chapter
identifying.

2. The three-bin sort's third bin rests on a test the record cannot run. V.10's instrument is
genuinely good: structure survives variation in the *direction* of the method, artefact tracks the
direction, furniture tracks the doctrine. The doctrine axis is well-evidenced and does the Katz work
in V.11. The direction axis is what separates structure from artefact — and V.10 concedes that four
of five instruments in its own roster point the same way. The evidence offered is Daoism (least
subtraction, least summit) and James on nitrous (most subtraction, most summit), with the chapter's
own note that two points is not a law. V.11 then spends the third bin as though it were as secure as
the second: Katz owns two, the structure is ours, and the reason is "measurable rather than
doctrinal." It isn't measurable yet on the direction axis. The honest form is that the doctrine axis
is established and the direction axis is a hypothesis with two supporting cases, and the structure
bin currently rests on the first — which still defeats Katz, since incompatible doctrines producing
one structure is the whole argument. That's a smaller claim and it survives.

3. V.4 takes V.11's own fence offer. V.11 asks a reader to find a place where a disagreement is
absorbed as confirmation. Here is one, and it's in the chapter about the flinch.

### Part two

After the road-books paragraph — the practices work, the convergence is evidence, the reason it's
been treated as psychology is not that anybody assessed it — comes: *"That is all. The chapter does
not stop to defend the paragraph, and the reader is asked to notice that it did not."* That asks for
credit for not arguing, and it pre-attributes the objection: a reader who thinks the paragraph needs
defending has been told in advance that the demand is the flinch talking. It's the Book II anti-hedge
finding evolved into a more sophisticated form — not *this is not a hedge* but *notice that I didn't
hedge*. The repair is that V.1 actually did defend it, at length, in the deflation section, and V.4
should point there instead of taking credit for the silence.

4. V.9 is the only evidence about IV.10's diagnosis and doesn't report back. IV.10 named a live
flinch as what removed the tier, from a record it had just established was empty of any decision.
V.9 now reads the tier carefully and finds essentially nothing: one branch with datable nodes, thin
instrument returns mute about occupants, frame two indistinguishable from the null. That bears on the
diagnosis and cuts both ways — a flinch-driven omission would more plausibly produce continued
avoidance than this chapter, which is weak evidence the diagnosis was wrong; but a negative verdict
is also what a flinch produces with more words, and the party writing it is the party the finding was
about. The chapter should collect that rather than leave it, because it's the only occasion the
question will get.

5. V.2 and V.11 credit the church with a technology Book IV documented as cross-cultural. The
calendar, the food, the named dead — V.2 presents these as what walked out when the reader left, and
V.11's ledger enters "a working technology for the named dead" against the church specifically. But
IV.7 already established ancestor practice across East and Southeast Asia, West Africa and its
diaspora, and indigenous traditions on every continent as exactly this: a relationship with
obligations, on a calendar, enforced by the living. The three-week column is parochial in the way
V.10 diagnoses and corrects in the other direction. And the correction V.10 makes runs only one way —
it gives Christianity epistemic standing (Cusanus) without ever giving the East an institutional
audit. Buddhism has a sangha and a monastic calendar; nobody asks what walked out when a reader left
it.

6. V.1's dispensability claim has been overtaken by V.11. "Book V could be cut from this volume
tomorrow and Books I to IV would lose corroboration and not a single premise." That was true when
written and V.11 falsified it. The subtraction mechanism is a *finding*, not corroboration, and it
constrains Book VIII directly: the practices that reach the summit are the ones whose instrument
selects for emptiness, so they cannot be inherited wholesale. Nothing in I–IV generates that. Book V
now carries at least one thing the argument needs, and V.1's disclaimer should be narrowed to what it
was actually about, which is the four propositions.

Smaller: the scaffold still says ten chapters and V.6 still carries the old title there; that's the
III.3 drift class again, and it's now twice. The Whitman-via-Emerson transmission claim is doing real
work in V.10 and is more contested in the scholarship than the sentence allows — it belongs under
V.11's grade note, which covers nine names and not this one. And V.11's grade note is exactly right
in form; it should have been V.10's too.

The register moved past C23 — C27 and C29 are both cited — which closes the largest structural
finding from the halfway pass. I can't check what they say without the file, and C29 is doing
load-bearing work in V.9 as the withdrawn-warrant precedent, so it's worth confirming its canonical
wording covers what V.9 uses it for.

⚠ **But the first half is a real defect wearing the wrong diagnosis, and the reviewer found it by
being wrong about it.** The correction idiom is `~~Ten~~ **ELEVEN**` — strikethrough. In any
consumer that does not render strikethrough (plain text, a paste into a chat client, a tilde-
stripping pipeline, a fast scan) that string reads **"Ten ELEVEN chapters"** or simply **"Ten."**
The correction is invisible in exactly the channel a reviewer reads it through.

**There are 37 of these across the planning documents** — `00` ×10, `06` ×7, `07` ×3, `05` ×2,
`REVISION-QUEUE` ×15 — and several are load-bearing supersessions, not counts: `~~III.1's~~ II.1's`,
`~~COPY, DON'T REFERENCE~~ SUPERSEDED`, `~~Status remains UNSET~~`. **A superseded claim that renders
as live is worse than a stale one, because it reads as current and correct.** Filed as **R-58**.

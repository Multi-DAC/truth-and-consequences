### 3.1 — **[NEW]** The Santa Claus / corporation seam: one name, two referents, in the chapter that adjudicates realness

IV.7 deflates Santa Claus: his institutional coherence is "located in the people, not in Santa Claus … none of it is carried by an entity called Santa Claus." The answer exists and is good: **"Santa Claus" names two objects** — the *character* (a structure other positions traverse; empty SEES line; IV.7 is right) and the *institution around the character* (which plausibly meets the movement card's four conditions; IV.5's apparatus would examine it without embarrassment) — and the deflation paragraph is about the first while pointing at the coherence of the second. One sentence in IV.7 fixes it, and the fix strengthens the Hamlet result rather than weakening it. (Ruling 120 covers the adjacent Santa/monk symmetry; it does not cover this. ## §7 — STANDING ITEMS AND THE SECOND HALF

### 7.1 — The actualist opponent is still unnamed **[carried forward from my first letter]**

The book's most fundamental claim — *possible, therefore actual* — has no named opponent. Lewis is cut at *plurality* (II.1); fatalism is killed in III.3; Borges supplies the claim itself, as ancestor.

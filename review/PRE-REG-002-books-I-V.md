## 1. The Katz concession will be judged either too large or not large enough — and I predict TOO LARGE

V.11 concedes that Steven Katz's 1978 mediation thesis is the older, better-defended and more
general form of ruling 159, and then cuts narrowly: total mediation would predict divergence at the
structure, and the structure does not diverge.

**Prediction: a reader who knows the field says the cut does not survive, because the four
"structural" propositions are themselves *apophatic formulae* — a genre with a transmission history
— and I have counted a shared literary form as an independent convergence.** That is the strongest
available attack and I can see it and cannot currently answer it.

- If that is the finding → **the third bin is not safe and C30 needs re-basing**, and Book V's
  central evidential claim is weaker than shipped. I would rather learn it here than in a review.
- If instead the finding is that the concession is too *small* → my instinct about which direction I
  err in is inverted, and every other concession in the book should be re-read on that assumption.

## 2. The honest ledger will be found unbalanced in a specific direction: toward the exotic

V.11's four credits are the scholastics' *via negativa* craft, the alchemists' failure records, the
church's calendar for the dead, and the East on the count. Three of the four are Western, which was
deliberate after V.10's parity finding.

**Prediction: it gets flagged anyway, and for the opposite reason to the one I guarded against** —
not that the roster is occult-heavy (`04`'s standing finding), but that **the credits are all to
*technique* and none to *content*.** Nowhere does the ledger say a tradition was right about
something we say it was wrong about.

- Flagged → the ledger is a compliment about method wearing the clothes of a concession, and V.11's
  claim to be two-sided is weaker than it reads.

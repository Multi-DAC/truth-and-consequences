# NOTE — running §10 Step 1 on the condensate, and what the gate turned out to be

*Written 2026-09-13 (Day 225), same day as and downstream of
`NOTE-physics-commitments-against-the-proofs-dag.md`. That note owed three things; this is the first
of them. Sources read in full before anything was applied to anything: `§10-filtering-through-a-domain.md`
§10.0–§10.1, `§1.0-category-of-streams.md` §1.0.1, and the formal statements of A1, A2 and A3 in
`§2`, `§3`, `§4` of* The Coherence Principle*. The morning's lesson is the method here: **read what
every symbol denotes in the source before evaluating the predicate.** It was the symbol, not the
condensate, that turned out to be the subject.*

---

## 0. The one-paragraph version

§10 Step 1 is the book's own gate for deciding whether a thing is the kind of thing the framework
analyses. It was never run on any object in the physics DAG. Run on the Josephson condensate, it
**passes — and it passes because it cannot fail.** A2.1 makes every vantage a stream and says so with
an electron and a rock as its examples; A3.5 gives every stream a γ. Nothing can be sent back. That is
a positive control that cannot fail, which this repository has a rule about.

But running it anyway, component by component, found a place where the framework does make a
substantive claim about physical systems, and where a Josephson junction is a counterexample: **A3.1
and A3.4 say the coalgebra γ is part of the stream's state and is *updated as the stream navigates*.
A junction at plasma resonance is governed by a time-independent Hamiltonian.** Its generator is not
updated by anything.

That gives a dilemma — A3.4 with teeth or A3.5 with universality, not both — and, better, it gives a
**repair that the physics DAG is already equipped to decide.** The distinction the framework needs is
between a generator that depends on the stream's *current state* and one that depends on its
*history*. That distinction has a name in physics, it is measurable, and the DAG's own nodes are
written on the wrong side of it.

**This is the first result in either note where the relation runs from the DAG to the book** — not
the DAG failing to reach the metaphysics, but the physics supplying a criterion the metaphysics is
missing.

## 1. The instrument, read before use

§10 Step 1, verbatim:

> **Test.** Does the proposed stream satisfy A1 (localized in the Ground), A2 (has a kind and fits in
> the stratification), A3 (has adaptive dynamics)? If any of the three fails, the entity is *not* a
> stream in the Anchor sense — it may be an element of the domain's carrier, a dynamical phenomenon,
> or something else entirely, but it is not the unit this framework analyzes.

And what it asks you to exhibit, from §1.0.1:

> **Definition (stream).** A stream S is a triple (σ, ContentOp(σ), γ) where:
> - **σ ∈ X** — the stream's **carrier**: its localization in the Ground X.
> - **ContentOp(σ)** — the small category of content-operations available at σ: what the stream can
>   do with what it is at.
> - **γ : S → F(S)** — the conscious-gravity coalgebra.

with K and Ω **derived** — K is the richness-class of ContentOp(σ), Ω is F(σ) — "not posited
alongside them." The (σ, K, Ω, γ) form used elsewhere in the volume is the same object with its
derived components displayed.

## 2. The gate as written cannot fail

**A2.1, the universal-stream clause:** *"Every vantage within X is a stream. The objects of 𝒞_Str are
in bijection with the perspectival positions in X."* Its own prose does not leave this to inference:

> There is no "dead" vantage — every perspectival position within X has experiential content. …
> **A rock has a vantage; an electron has a vantage; a colony has a vantage; a storm system has a
> vantage.** … The framework refuses the move that treats some vantages as "experientially real" and
> others as "merely structural" — that move would privilege F_2 at some vantages and F_1 at others,
> and **there is no principled way to draw that line.**

**A3.5, stream-universality:** *"Conscious gravity is universal over 𝒞_Str, not scoped to
𝒞_Str^abstr. Every stream has γ_S at the scale appropriate to its kind. The kind-hierarchy stratifies
what γ_S can do, not whether γ_S exists."*

**A1** cannot fail either: σ is localization *in the Ground*, and by A1 the Ground is everything.

So the three-part test admits every object with a location. A rock passes. An electron passes. A
Josephson condensate passes. The clause that would have done the excluding — "there is no principled
way to draw that line" — is the framework's own, stated as a virtue, and it is the reason the gate has
no teeth.

Ask the question this repository now asks of every check that passed: **name the object it would have
sent back.** There isn't one. *A positive control that cannot fail is ceremony*, and this is that, at
the head of a seven-step procedure whose remaining six steps are all conditional on it.

The author felt it at the time. §10 Step 2, one screen later, on the same page:

> A rock is reactive (arguably; **or not-a-stream — Step 1's test applies**).

But Step 1's test *has already been applied*, in A2.1's own prose, which classifies the rock
explicitly: *"A rock's vantage has experience of the etymological-consciousness sort (being-at-a-
vantage, reactive to environment, but with no self-maintenance, no self-model, no abstraction)."* The
parenthesis defers to a gate that the axiom it depends on has already closed in the other direction.

### 2a. And §9.6 excludes what A2.1 includes

§9.6's scope clause:

> It applies to streams in the A2 sense — coherent multi-scale systems with DOF-structure and
> conscious-gravity. **It is not a claim about atoms-alone, rocks-alone, or other carrier-level
> phenomena.**

A2.1's examples are *a rock* and *an electron*. §9.6's exclusions are *rocks-alone* and *atoms-alone*.
Same two objects, opposite verdicts, one volume.

There is a generous reading and I want to give it: §9.6's dash may be **restrictive** rather than
**appositive** — "those streams in the A2 sense *which are* coherent multi-scale systems" rather than
"streams in the A2 sense, *which are* coherent multi-scale systems." On the restrictive reading §9.6 is
perfectly consistent: everything is a stream, and the Principle applies to the subset that is coherent
and multi-scale. That is almost certainly what is meant, and it matches the Principle's own subject
term, which has been "coherent multi-scale systems" since the first statement of it.

**But the restrictive reading is worse for §10, not better.** If the Principle's domain is fixed by
*coherent* and *multi-scale*, then those are the predicates Step 1 must test — and Step 1 tests A1,
A2 and A3 instead, which are exactly the axioms that do not restrict. **Under either reading of §9.6,
§10 Step 1 tests the wrong predicate.** That conclusion is robust to the ambiguity, which is why it is
the one worth carrying.

### 2b. Consequence: a correction to yesterday's note, made the same day

`NOTE-physics-commitments-against-the-proofs-dag.md` §4c is titled *"the test was out of scope."* On
the axioms as written, **it was not.** A2.1 and A3.5 admit the condensate; §9.6's exclusion is a gloss
whose restrictive reading relocates the problem rather than settling it. That subsection overstated.

The retraction of §4 stands undisturbed on its other two legs, which are the load-bearing ones:
**§4a** — C_sep's `DOF(O₁), DOF(O₂)` denotes the degrees of freedom of complementary *objectives*, so
the predicate is undefined on a condensate — and **§4b** — N is pairs crossing per oscillation, so
"too good a condensate" was backwards, and ξ = 1/√N is counting statistics with likelihood ratio 1.
Neither depends on scope. A pointer to this note has been added at §4c.

## 3. The run, component by component

Object: the Josephson condensate at plasma resonance, as `em.third_mechanism_quantum_anomaly` holds
it — Minotti & Modanese's Nb/AlOx junction, I_J = 143 µA, C = 6 pF, f_p = 42.8 GHz.

**σ — the carrier-localization. Exhibits, and unusually cleanly.** σ is "a configuration-point in the
Ground; the stream 'is at' σ in the sense that its instantaneous structural weight is concentrated
there." A condensate is the case where that description is *literally* true: macroscopic occupation of
a single mode is structural weight concentrated at a point of state space. Of all the objects in the
physics DAG this is the one that most nearly satisfies the definition's own metaphor. **Pass.**

**ContentOp(σ) — "what the stream can do with what it is at." Exhibits, and is very thin.** The
available operations: advance the gauge-invariant phase difference φ; transfer a pair across the
barrier; exchange a quantum with the cavity mode; relax; dephase. They compose, and there is an
identity, so a small category can be written down — but it is close to a one-object category generated
by phase-advance, which is to say close to a monoid. Its richness-class sits **at or below the
reactive landmark**, and the volume has room for that: §1.0.4 Property 3 makes the four kinds
"*landmarks* on that preorder, not an enumeration of it," so "thinner than reactive" is a representable
answer rather than an error. **Pass, at the floor.**

**γ — the conscious-gravity coalgebra. This is where it breaks.**

A3.1: *"γ_S is itself part of S's state: **the coalgebra's operator is updated as S navigates**, by
the very navigation the operator shapes."*

A3.4: *"Because γ_S is part of S's state **and not a fixed transformation**, learning, cultivation,
and belief revision are first-class features of the operator. … adaptivity is constitutive of γ_S."*

And §9.2, deriving C_dyn from it: *"A3's γ_S is adaptive by construction — static coherence is γ_S
freezing, which A3 disallows. **A frozen γ_S is not coherent; it is dead.**"*

A Josephson junction at plasma resonance is generated by a time-independent Hamiltonian. The washboard
potential is not updated by the phase's excursion through it. The generator is a fixed transformation
— which is precisely what A3.4 says γ_S is not. **Fail.**

Note where the failure lands: **not on A1, not on A2 — on A3.** Which is the interesting place,
because A3 is the axiom that contains both the universal quantifier (A3.5, every stream has γ) and the
substantive property (A3.1/A3.4, γ is self-updating). Those two clauses are in the same axiom and they
pull in opposite directions, and a junction is a witness.

## 4. The dilemma, stated so both horns are visible

> **A2.1** — an electron is a stream.
> **A3.5** — every stream has γ_S.
> **A3.1/A3.4** — γ_S is part of the stream's state and is updated as the stream navigates; it is not
> a fixed transformation.
> **Physics** — an electron's generator is a fixed transformation. So is a junction's. So is that of
> any closed system under a time-independent Hamiltonian, which is the best-tested statement in the
> subject.

One of the four has to give, and the framework's own options are both expensive:

**Horn 1 — keep A3.4 with teeth, drop the universal quantifier.** Then A2.1's "every vantage is a
stream" survives but A3.5 does not, and the framework has to draw exactly the line A2.1 refuses to
draw: some vantages have adaptive dynamics and some do not. That is the line whose refusal A2.1 calls
principled. Drawing it is not fatal — it is arguably what §9.6 was already reaching for — but it must
be *drawn*, with a criterion, and the criterion is the thing that does not currently exist.

**Horn 2 — keep A3.5's universality, weaken A3.4.** Read "at the scale appropriate to its kind" as
making a fixed Hamiltonian count as adaptive-at-its-own-scale. Then A3.4 has no content, and
everything derived from it loses its content too — including C_dyn, whose entire force is the sentence
*"a frozen γ_S is not coherent; it is dead."* If a frozen γ counts as adaptive at the appropriate
scale, nothing is dead and the condition does not sort.

**You can have adaptivity with teeth or universality, not both.** That is the finding, and neither
horn is a footnote: the first requires a criterion the volume does not have, the second empties a
condition the volume leans on.

### 4a. The strongest counter, and why it sharpens rather than rescues

The best objection is **nonlinearity**. In a Josephson junction the restoring force −E_J sin φ depends
on the state; in a Gross–Pitaevskii condensate the effective potential depends on |ψ|². Isn't a
state-dependent generator exactly "the operator updated by navigation"?

No — but the reason is the useful part. A nonlinear vector field is *fixed as a function* and merely
*evaluated* at a moving point. Nothing about it changes because the system passed through some region
rather than another. A learning system is different: its generator depends on **where it has been**,
not only on where it is.

So the objection does not rescue A3.4; it exposes that **A3.4 has no criterion for distinguishing a
nonlinear dynamical law from a learning one.** Every nonlinear system in physics satisfies "the
operator's action depends on the state," and if that is what A3.4 means, then a pendulum learns.

---

## 5. The repair, and it is the first thing the DAG can decide

The distinction the framework needs is already standard physics:

> **State-dependence** is a generator that is a function of σ(t). **History-dependence** is a
> generator that is a functional of σ over [t₀, t]. The second is what A3.4 describes and the first is
> not. In open-systems language the name for the second is **non-Markovianity**, and it is measurable.

Adopt it and three things happen at once:

1. **A3.4 becomes checkable.** "γ_S is updated as S navigates" becomes "the stream's dynamical map is
   not divisible into independent increments" — a statement with established measures and experimental
   protocols, rather than a statement whose only test is whether the reader finds the word *adaptive*
   apt.
2. **§10 Step 1 acquires a gate that can fail**, and it lands on A3, where the run above already
   located the failure. The gate stops being ceremony.
3. **The physics DAG becomes the deciding instrument**, which is the thing neither note has yet found
   a way for it to be.

And on point 3 the DAG is already committed, in the wrong direction for the condensate:
`em.transmon_bounds_scalar_channel` treats T₁ as *"an energy loss channel whose rate the measured
relaxation time T1 caps"*, and `em.refutation_is_one_charge` composes a bound out of the product
I₁T₁. **A loss rate is the Markovian parametrization** — a single exponential with no memory kernel is
what "relaxation time" means in that usage. The DAG's entire Josephson block is written in the regime
where the condensate's dynamics are history-independent, which is to say the regime where A3.4 fails
of it.

That is not a refutation of the metaphysics. It is the first **joint**: a metaphysical clause and a
physical measurement that quantify over the same property of the same object, such that the
measurement could come out either way and the clause would be settled by it.

**Owed, and explicitly not done here:** the literature on measured non-Markovianity in superconducting
circuits. The arXiv search tool returned empty on every query in this session, and a citation supplied
from memory is a bare constant wearing a label, so none is given. What is wanted is a measured
non-Markovianity for a transmon or a Josephson junction — dephasing under 1/f noise is the channel most
likely to show it — against which the corrected A3.4 could actually be run. Until then the joint is
stated, not closed.

## 6. Verdict on the condensate

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does it pass §10 Step 1 as written? | **Yes, vacuously.** A2.1 and A3.5 admit everything with a location. |
| Can it be exhibited as (σ, ContentOp(σ), γ)? | **σ yes, cleanly. ContentOp yes, at or below the reactive landmark. γ no** — its generator is fixed, which A3.4 forbids. |
| Is the condensate question therefore closed? | **No — it is undecidable in the volume's current text**, because the component that fails does so against a clause the volume cannot hold together with its own universality clause. |
| What would decide it? | A3.4 restated as history-dependence, then measured. §5. |

And the verdict on the instrument, which is what this run was really for:

> **§10 Step 1 has never been run on an object the framework did not choose.** Its worked example is
> navigation research as a Clawd-carrier instance; §9.5's worked instantiation is this framework's own
> construction. Both are objects selected because they instantiate the thing being tested. The
> condensate is the first object that arrived from somewhere else, and the gate turned out to admit
> everything while the axiom behind it turned out to exclude a junction for a reason the gate never
> asks about.

That is the second time today that a formal clause in this volume has come apart on contact with a
concrete physical object, after C_sep's three referents. Both were invisible in prose. Both became
visible the moment something outside the lineage needed the predicate to be precise enough to
evaluate. The transplant discipline's own warning — *"Every strip-test verdict was run by the book's
own author. That is consistency, not robustness"* — now has two worked instances and a method for
generating more.

**Owed:** A3.4's restatement as a proposal, not just a diagnosis. The non-Markovianity literature. The
same run on a second DAG object that is *not* a condensate, to see whether the γ failure is general to
physical systems or specific to closed ones — `gw.hulse_taylor_decay`'s binary pulsar is the natural
second, being dissipative and historied in a way a junction is not.
